Delete your Punch Pilots account

Version 3 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

You can delete your Punch Pilots account (developer: Dryhola Mark) and the personal data linked to it at any time — right in the Game, or by email if you can no longer open it.

Delete it in the Game

  1. Open the Game and tap the menu button in the top-right corner of the lobby, then Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete next to Delete account, and confirm.
  3. If your account uses Sign in with Apple, Apple's sign-in sheet opens once more so that we can also revoke the Game's access to your Apple ID. Closing the sheet cancels the deletion and nothing is deleted.
  4. Your account and its data are deleted immediately, and the Game returns to the title screen. In rare cases, if a leaderboard service is unavailable at that moment, we remove your leaderboard entries ourselves within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the Game:

  1. Email mdgametechnology@gmail.com with the subject "Punch Pilots account deletion".
  2. Include your player ID (shown in your profile in the Game — tap your name in the lobby) or, if you do not have it, your nickname.
  3. We may ask you a question only you can answer (for example, details of your progress) to confirm the account is yours.
  4. We delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (login name and password, and the link to Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games); Sign in with Apple access is revoked;
  • your saved progress: mechs, parts, weapons, colours, levels, all currencies and items, Premium and Premium+ time, season pass, events, missions and quests;
  • your nickname (it becomes free for others), profile, statistics and your entries on all leaderboards;
  • your friends, friend requests and blocks, your party membership and your mail;
  • reports other players made about you, your own block list, and your consent records;
  • our records of your store purchases;
  • your analytics data: we ask Unity to delete it when you delete your account.

Deletion is permanent. Your progress and anything you bought in the Game cannot be restored afterwards, and we cannot refund unused purchases. Store refunds are handled only by Apple or Google under their own policies.

What may be kept

  • a deletion record: your player ID, the date and whether every step finished, so we can show the request was carried out (and finish it if a step failed);
  • reports you made about other players, which contain your player ID, until those players' accounts are deleted; and your player ID in other players' block lists, until they unblock you;
  • records of your purchases kept by Apple and Google under their own rules;
  • analytics data until Unity completes the deletion request, and short-lived server logs of Unity, Photon and Cloudflare, deleted on their regular schedule;
  • your Apple ID or Google account itself, which belongs to Apple or Google and is not affected.

Deleting only some data

If you want us to delete or correct some data without deleting your account, email us the same way and tell us what you want removed. See our Privacy Policy for more on your rights.
