Delete your Punch Pilots account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

You can delete your Punch Pilots account (developer: Dryhola Mark) and the personal data linked to it at any time — right in the Game, or by email if you can no longer open it.

Delete it in the Game

  1. Open the Game and tap the menu button in the top-right corner of the lobby, then Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete next to Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted immediately, and the Game returns to the title screen.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the Game:

  1. Open the Game and copy your player ID from your profile (tap your name in the lobby). If you can no longer open the Game, give us your username or nickname instead.
  2. Email mdgametechnology@gmail.com with the subject "Punch Pilots account deletion" and include your player ID or username.
  3. We may ask you a question only you can answer (for example, details of your progress) to confirm the account is yours.
  4. We delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (username and password);
  • your saved progress: mechs, parts, weapons, colors, levels, currencies, Premium time, season pass and missions;
  • your nickname, profile, statistics, your entries on all leaderboards, your block list and reports other players made about you;
  • your consent records, once the period the law requires has passed.

Deletion is permanent. Your progress and anything you bought in the Game cannot be restored or refunded afterwards.

What may be kept

  • Records of real-money purchases, for as long as tax and consumer-protection law requires (usually up to 5 years);
  • analytics and diagnostic data that our providers keep for a limited time (up to 24 months) or in a form that no longer identifies you;
  • short-lived server logs of our providers, deleted on their regular schedule;
  • a record that the account was deleted and when (player ID and date only), so we can show the request was carried out.

Deleting only some data

If you want us to delete or correct some data without deleting your account, email us the same way and tell us what you want removed. See our Privacy Policy for more on your rights.
