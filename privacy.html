Privacy Policy

Version 3 · Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Punch Pilots mobile game and its online services (the "Game") collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the choices you have. The Game is operated by Dryhola Mark, an individual entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Ukraine ("we", "us", "our"), who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: mdgametechnology@gmail.com.

2. When collection starts

When you first start the Game, it asks you to accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. Until you accept, the Game creates no account, does not connect to our online services and does not start analytics or advertising. The Unity engine itself may send Unity anonymous hardware statistics (such as device model, operating system and graphics chip) when the app starts; they contain no account or player ID.

After you accept, the Game creates your account, starts analytics and connects a real-time channel used for friend and party updates. At the same time, on iOS it shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; then it starts the advertising SDK and asks for permission to send notifications. If you change device or reinstall, you are asked to accept again for the account you use.

4. What other players can see

  • Your nickname, mech, weapon and rank are shown to other players in matches, in the kill feed and in match results.
  • Your nickname, trophies and mech appear on the season leaderboard, and your nickname and score on event leaderboards.
  • Any player can open your public profile, which shows your nickname, mech, season level, highest trophies, game statistics, when you started playing and when you were last active.
  • Any player can find you by your exact nickname or your player ID (there is no browsing or partial search).
  • Your friends see whether you are online, whether you are in the lobby or in a battle, and when you were last seen.
  • Members of your party see your nickname, mech, weapon, ready status and Battle Express progress.

5. Advertising

The Game shows only optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch an ad to receive an in-game reward. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, its ad-quality tool, and the Unity Ads network. These partners may collect and use device and advertising identifiers, your IP address and ad-interaction data to select, deliver, limit the frequency of, measure and protect ads, and they process this data under their own privacy policies (section 8).

  • iOS: the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps. If you decline, you still see the same optional ads, but they are not based on cross-app tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: the Game declares the advertising ID permission and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad permissions so that our ad partners can use your Google Advertising ID and Android's privacy-preserving ad features. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads); after you delete it, ads are no longer based on that identifier.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on iOS, personalized ads are used only if you allow tracking. On Android, right after you accept the Terms and this Policy, the Game asks separately whether ads may be personalized; they are personalized only if you choose “Allow”. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Personalized ads. Analytics is based on our legitimate interests (section 9); you can object by contacting us, and we will delete your analytics data.
  • Premium+ gives the rewards that otherwise need an ad — the doubled battle reward, extra roulette spins, extra mystery chest opens and the day's extra event tickets — without watching an ad (the daily limits stay the same). Regular Premium does not remove ads.

6. Notifications and in-game messages

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a daily reward or a free spin is ready. These are created locally on your device; we do not use a push-notification server and no message content is sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

While the Game is open, our server sends it real-time updates about your friends and party (for example, an invitation or a change in the party). These are not notifications on your device and stop when you close the Game.

7. Safety, moderation and automated decisions

  • Nicknames are checked automatically when they are set (length, characters, offensive words, personal data such as emails or phone numbers, reserved names and names already taken).
  • When three different players report a nickname, it is automatically replaced with a neutral name (PLAYER_XXXX) in your profile, in matches and on the season leaderboard (event leaderboards show the new name after your next event battle), and you are told why in the Game. You can choose a new nickname.
  • Blocking a player also notifies us, as the app stores require (up to 20 reports and blocks a day).
  • Match loadouts and purchases are checked automatically against your saved progress and the store's records to prevent cheating and fraud.
  • When we restrict your account, the Game shows you the decision, the reason and a case number; when your nickname is reset, the Game tells you why. You can ask for a review by a person by emailing us with the case number or your player ID.

9. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract — running the Game, your account, matches, friends, parties, mail, events, purchases and saved progress.
  • Legitimate interests — security, anti-cheat and fraud prevention, acting on reports and automated nickname checks, fixing bugs and improving the Game through analytics. You can object to this processing by contacting us, and we will delete your analytics data.
  • Consent — personalized advertising and cross-app tracking (on iOS, through App Tracking Transparency) and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time as described in sections 5 and 6, or by contacting us.
  • Legal obligation — keeping records and handling notices and statements of reasons that the law requires (including the EU Digital Services Act).

10. International transfers

We are located in Ukraine. Unity Technologies, Cloudflare, Apple and Google process data in the United States and other countries; Exit Games (Photon) is based in Germany and uses servers in several regions (you are usually connected to the server closest to you). Where data leaves the EEA or UK, these providers rely on adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where they are certified, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data (profile, progress, currencies, items, consent records, mail, block list) — while your account exists. When you delete your account in the Game, it is deleted at once; when you ask by email, within 30 days. We may delete guest accounts that have not been used for more than 24 months.
  • Mail — each letter expires after at most 30 days.
  • Party data — while the party exists. A party nobody has used for 30 minutes ends, and its record is removed when its members next open the Game or leave it.
  • Reports about a player — the latest 100 reports are kept until that player's account is deleted.
  • Leaderboard entries — kept until your account is deleted. Past season and event boards are not cleared automatically, and record boards keep your best result.
  • Analytics data — kept by Unity under its retention rules; when you delete your account in the Game, we also ask Unity to delete your analytics data.
  • Server logs of Unity, Photon and Cloudflare — kept by those providers for short periods for security and operations.
  • Purchase records — Apple and Google keep their own records of your store purchases under their rules. We do not keep our own copy after your account is deleted.
  • Deletion record — after deletion we keep only your player ID, the date and whether every step finished, to show that the request was carried out.
  • Data on your device (session token, settings, language) — until you sign out or uninstall the Game.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email mdgametechnology@gmail.com from the address you want us to answer, and include your player ID (shown in your profile in the Game). We answer within 30 days. In the EEA and UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. California and other US states

Residents of California and some other US states have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete and correct it, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information for money. Our ad partners may use advertising identifiers for cross-context advertising; to stop this, decline tracking on iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) or delete your advertising ID on Android (Settings → Privacy → Ads). For any other request, email us with your player ID.

14. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the Game (Settings → Account → Delete account), or by email if you can no longer open the Game. The steps, and what is deleted and kept, are on our account deletion page. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the Game stops further collection but does not delete your account.

15. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 16 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), you may use the Game only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without the required consent, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with providers that use industry-standard security. Purchases and rewards are granted only by our server. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

17. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the version and "Last updated" date and, for material changes, ask you to accept the new version in the Game.
