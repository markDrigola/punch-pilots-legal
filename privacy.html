Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Punch Pilots mobile game and its online services (the "Game") collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The Game is operated by Dryhola Mark, an individual entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Ukraine ("we", "us", "our"), who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: mdgametechnology@gmail.com.

1. When collection starts

When you first start the Game, it asks you to accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy. Until you accept, the Game does not contact our servers, creates no account, does not start analytics or advertising, and shows no system prompts. Only after you accept does the Game create your account and start analytics, show the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS, then start the advertising SDK and ask for permission to send notifications.

3. What other players can see

Your nickname, rank, mech and loadout are shown to other players in matches. Your nickname, trophies and mech appear on the public season leaderboard. Any player can open your public profile, which shows your nickname, rank, trophies and game statistics and when you were last active.

4. Advertising

The Game shows only optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch an ad to receive an in-game reward. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation and the Unity Ads network. These partners may collect and use device and advertising identifiers to select, deliver, limit the frequency of and measure ads, and they process this data under their own privacy policies.

  • iOS: the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not based on cross-app tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we ask for your consent before analytics and advertising start: you accept the Terms and this Policy on first launch, and on iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt separately governs access to your advertising identifier. Where consent for personalized ads has not been given, ads are shown non-personalized. You can withdraw your consent at any time in your device's tracking and ads settings, or by contacting us.
  • Premium players receive ad rewards without watching an ad.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a daily reward or a free spin is ready. These are created locally on your device; no message content is sent to us. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract — running the Game, your account, matches, purchases and saved progress.
  • Legitimate interests — security, anti-cheat, fixing bugs and improving the Game through analytics. You can object to this processing by contacting us.
  • Consent — personalized advertising and cross-app tracking (on iOS, through App Tracking Transparency) and notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings or by contacting us.
  • Legal obligation — keeping records that the law requires.

8. International transfers

Our service providers process data in several countries, including the United States, the European Union and the regions where Photon and Cloudflare run their servers (you are usually connected to the server closest to you). Where data leaves the EEA or UK, we rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses used by these providers.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data — while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it within 30 days. We may delete guest accounts that have not been used for more than 24 months.
  • Analytics and diagnostic data — kept by Unity under its retention rules, and no longer than 24 months in a form that identifies you.
  • Server logs of Photon and Cloudflare — kept by those providers for short periods for security and operations.
  • Purchase and consent records — as long as the law requires (for example, tax and consumer-protection rules).
  • Data on your device (session token, settings, language) — until you sign out or uninstall the Game.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email mdgametechnology@gmail.com from the address you want us to answer, and include your player ID (shown in your profile in the Game). We answer within 30 days. In the EEA and UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell personal information. Ad partners may use advertising identifiers for cross-context advertising; you can limit this with the device settings described in section 4.

11. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, even without the Game installed. The steps are on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your account, saved progress, purchases, nickname and leaderboard entry, and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the Game stops further collection but does not delete your account.

12. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are under 16 (or under the age of digital consent in your country), you may use the Game only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal data without the required consent, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with providers that use industry-standard security. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data. If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, ask you to accept the new version in the Game.
