Support

We usually answer within 2 business days.

Contact us

Write to mdgametechnology@gmail.com. To help us find your account, include your nickname and your Player ID (in the game: tap your name in the lobby; it is also shown on the Friends screen), your device model and what happened.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. If something you bought did not arrive, open Settings → Restore purchases in the game first. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (Google Play refund help) under their policies.

Lost progress

A guest account is tied to your device. Link Apple, Google or a login and password in Settings to keep your progress when you delete the app or change your phone. A guest account that was not saved cannot be restored. If you still have its Player ID, write to us and we will check what we can do.

Report a player

Use Report on the player's profile in the game; you can also Block them: their name is hidden from you, they are removed from your friends, and their friend requests and party invites no longer reach you. For anything urgent, email us.

Delete your account

In the game: Settings → Delete account. Without the game: see Delete your account.
